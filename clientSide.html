Section 1

Executive Summary & Product Vision

Millions of people now turn to general-purpose AI chatbots for health advice. These tools can produce fluent but unverified answers, invent medical facts ("hallucinate"), and offer no consistent way to recognise an emergency. For a health organisation, that is a patient-safety and reputational risk.

Our platform replaces that uncertainty with a clinician-governed, deterministic triage framework. Every question, red-flag rule, and care recommendation is defined and approved by clinicians. It is not improvised by a language model at runtime. The same inputs always produce the same, explainable outcome, which can be audited, reviewed, and improved under clinical governance.

The Problem
  • Unverified, non-reproducible AI health advice
  • No guaranteed emergency recognition
  • Forced sign-ups that deter people from seeking help early
  • No usable handover for the treating clinician
Our Approach
  • Clinician-approved, rule-based safety logic
  • Immediate red-flag diversion to emergency guidance
  • Anonymous access with no account required
  • A doctor-ready consultation summary on every assessment
Core Values
Anonymous by Default

Zero mandatory sign-up. Anyone can start a symptom check within seconds, with no name, email, or phone number.

Structured Intake

Guided, clinically sequenced questions capture the right facts the first time, with no guesswork and no free-form drift.

Verifiable Medical Boundaries

The platform provides educational triage guidance, never a diagnosis, and states clearly when it cannot assess reliably.

Section 3

Clinical Safety Matrix & 5-Tier Care Acuity

Tier Care Destination Meaning & Platform Behaviour
1 Emergency Assistance Now Immediate emergency guidance with direct dialer access. Zero delays and no further questioning.
2 Urgent Medical Care Today Prompt same-day clinical evaluation recommended.
3 Arrange Medical Care Soon Routine clinical consultation advised; book at a convenient time.
4 General Self-Care Information Low-risk guidance, paired with safety-net indicators describing when to seek care.
5 Unable to Assess Reliably Pediatric boundary (children under 2 years) and ambiguous or insufficient inputs. The platform declines to guess and directs the person to a clinician.
Red-Flag Safety Guarantee

Life-threatening presentations, including chest pain patterns suggestive of heart attack, stroke warning signs, and acute breathing or consciousness distress, are evaluated before routine questioning. When a red-flag rule is met, the person is taken immediately to Emergency Assistance Now guidance. No further questions, no scoring, and no waiting.

Section 5

Privacy, Ethics & Data Sovereignty

Privacy-First Design

No names, phone numbers, or email addresses are required to check symptoms. Nothing personally identifying is needed to receive safety guidance.

Ephemeral Session Isolation

Responses exist only for the duration of the active evaluation. They are not retained, profiled, or monetised, and each session is isolated from every other.

Medical Disclaimer & Boundaries

The platform provides educational triage information only. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace the judgement of a licensed physician. In any emergency, or if the person is worried, they should contact local emergency services or a healthcare professional immediately.
